# Disclosure: archival material vs. illustration

- **Archival:** every patent drawing (Figs. 2, 6 and 7, the sheet header, and the inventors' names as lettered on the drawings) is reproduced from U.S. Patent 3,482,037 (USPTO). The drawings have been recoloured and cropped. Nothing has been redrawn or added inside them. The caricatured "visitor" figure in the patent's Fig. 1 and Fig. 2 was cropped out on purpose.
- **Illustrative reconstruction (AI-generated):** the slide 2 image (a 1960s apartment door at night) was generated in Midjourney from a settings-and-objects prompt with no people. It is not the Browns' home, not a documented scene and not a photograph. It is labeled "Illustrative reconstruction" on the slide, and the slide's source line says "AI-generated image, Midjourney." A second Midjourney take showing the door ajar was rejected because it implied an event that never happened.
- **Explanatory graphics:** the cutaway camera diagram (slide 3), the monitor silhouette and radio-wave marks (slide 4), the phone "live feed" (slide 1 animation) and the doorbell outline (slide 7) are explanatory graphics, not period artifacts.
- **AI generation is limited to the slide 2 setting.** No image of Marie or Albert Brown is shown or simulated. No photograph of the Browns was used because none was available under a clear licence. The known press photo is credited New York Times/Redux.
- **Suggested caption footnote:** "Patent drawings: U.S. Patent 3,482,037 (USPTO). Slide 2 is an AI-generated illustrative reconstruction, not a photograph."
