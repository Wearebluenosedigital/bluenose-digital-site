## Claims deliberately left out

- **Albert's irregular hours.** No reachable source confirmed it.
- **"The first modern home-security system."** I couldn't confirm Lemelson-MIT's exact wording, so the phrase appears nowhere.
- **The 1969 report that the system was "not yet in production" and that the Browns hoped to interest manufacturers and home builders.** I couldn't locate the original article. Slide 6 uses the more cautious wording "not widely manufactured."
- **The ~32% rise in Queens crime (1960–65), slow police response, and "cited in 35 patents."** These come only from S1 excerpts, so they are not used.
- **The audio tape recorder (P1).** It's accurate, but left off the slides to keep them readable. It appears in the captions.
